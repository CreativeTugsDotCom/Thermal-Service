System Replacement

When replacement makes sense, understand your options.

A new AC installation is a significant decision for any homeowner. It shouldn't be an automatic answer to every breakdown, but when old equipment becomes unreliable or inefficient, replacement is often the most practical long-term step.

We treat installation as a carefully considered service. The appropriate system depends entirely on your property's specifics and your cooling needs.

We will evaluate your current setup, discuss equipment options clearly without high-pressure sales tactics, and ensure the final installation meets rigorous technical standards for proper clearances and tidy operation.

Common Questions

What types of HVAC problems can I call about?
You can contact us if your AC is not cooling, your heater isn't working, the system is making unusual noises, or if you need a professional assessment of your current equipment.
Does Thermal Service repair air-conditioning systems?
Yes. Troubleshooting and repairing residential air-conditioning systems is a core service. We focus on identifying the specific issue and explaining the necessary fix.
Can I ask about a new AC installation or replacement?
Absolutely. If your system is old or a repair is not cost-effective, we can discuss appropriate replacement options tailored to your home without high-pressure sales.
Does Thermal Service handle commercial HVAC?
Our primary focus is residential service. If you have a small commercial property, please call us to discuss the equipment type and confirm if the project fits our capabilities.
What should I have ready when I call?
It helps to know exactly what the system is (or isn't) doing, where the equipment is located on your property, and your availability for a service visit.
